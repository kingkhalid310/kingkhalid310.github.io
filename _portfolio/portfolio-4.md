## Overview

Cerebral arteriolosclerosis is a common small-vessel pathology, but consistent criteria for identifying and characterizing it across clinical and research settings remain limited. This project aims to develop diagnostic criteria for arteriolosclerosis by integrating **neuropathology, neuroimaging, and clinical data**.

## Current focus

- Characterizing arteriolosclerosis using evidence from neuropathology reports and tissue-based assessment.
- Identifying corresponding MRI features and patterns of cerebral small-vessel disease.
- Evaluating relevant clinical and vascular-risk information.
- Building a transparent, reproducible framework for future validation in independent cohorts.

## Goal

The long-term goal is to improve the consistent identification of cerebral arteriolosclerosis and support research on its contribution to cognitive impairment, stroke, and mixed age-related brain pathologies.
